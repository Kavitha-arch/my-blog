The repetitive sound phrases (like ranat ranat ka... or kanat kanat ka...) are dynamic onomatopoeias and phonetics characteristic of Sanskrit stotrams. They are designed to rhythmically evoke the physical atmosphere, sounds, and cosmic vibrations of the scene being described.
Here is the exact linguistic and contextual breakdown of all the Sanskrit text labels and phonetic phrases found within each panel of the sketch.
------------------------------
## PANEL 1 (Verse 12.1): The Cosmic Battle## The Main Phonetic Phrase

* रणत् रणत् क... (Raṇat Raṇat Ka...):
* Meaning: "Clanging, ringing, or sounding repeatedly."
   * Context: It mimics the rhythmic, metallic clashing of weapons, swords, and armor on a roaring battlefield.

## Individual Labels (Right to Left)

* मल्लमल्लक (Malla-mallaka):
* Meaning: "Wrestler against wrestler" or "the chief of wrestlers."
   * Context: Points to the muscular demons engaged in hand-to-hand combat against the Goddess.
* वीर (Vīra):
* Meaning: "Warrior" or "hero."
   * Context: Labeling the valiant combatants engaged in the great war.
* चित्तचित्त (Chitta-chitta):
* Meaning: "Mind to mind" or "attentive concentration."
   * Context: Refers to the absolute, razor-sharp psychological focus required by warriors in the heat of a life-or-death battle.
* मदमद (Mada-mada):
* Meaning: "Intoxicated with pride/fury" or "raging."
   * Context: Describes the wild, arrogant fury of the demonic army charging ahead.

------------------------------
## PANEL 2 (Verse 12.2): The Serene Forest## The Main Phonetic Phrase

* लसत् लसत् क... (Lasat Lasat Ka...):
* Meaning: "Shining, playing, beautifully appearing, or blooming gracefully."
   * Context: Evokes the gentle, dancing movement of light filtering through trees and flowers swaying in the wind.

## Individual Labels (Right to Left)

* वल्लरी (Vallarī):
* Meaning: "Creeping vine" or "climbing plant."
   * Context: Labeling the lush, natural foliage wrapping around the forest trees and structures.
* मालती (Mālatī):
* Meaning: "Wild jasmine flowers."
   * Context: Pointing to the fragrant blooms that adorn the surrounding landscape.
* कुंजकोठरक (Kuñja-koṭharaka):
* Meaning: "Forest bower" or "hollow of a tree/arbor."
   * Context: Labeling the small, natural shelters and dwellings within the deep woods.
* किरात (Kirāta):
* Meaning: "Forest dweller," "mountain tribe," or "hunter."
   * Context: Refers to the simple, nature-worshipping tribal communities gathered around the Goddess.
* झिञ्झिक (Jhiñjhika):
* Meaning: "Cricket" or the "humming sound of crickets."
   * Context: Points to the peaceful, ambient sounds of nature filling the quiet forest air.

------------------------------
## PANEL 3 (Verse 12.3): The Radiant Form## The Main Phonetic Phrase

* कनत् कनत् क... (Kanat Kanat Ka...):
* Meaning: "Flashing, gleaming, glittering, or radiating light."
   * Context: Captures the brilliant, reflective flash of polished weapons and the blinding, golden aura emanating from the Goddess.

## Individual Labels (Right to Left)

* स्मित कोमल (Smita Komala):
* Meaning: "Tender, gentle, or soft smile."
   * Context: Emphasizes that despite holding weapons, her expression remains perfectly peaceful, innocent, and smiling.
* प्रफुल्ल (Praphulla):
* Meaning: "Fully blossomed" or "bursting into flower."
   * Context: Describes her beautiful form blooming with life, like a fresh flower.
* परिष्कृत (Pariṣkṛta):
* Meaning: "Highly polished," "refined," or "sharpened."
   * Context: Refers to the pristine, gleaming condition of her weapons and armor.
* कान्ति (Kānti):
* Meaning: "Radiance," "splendor," or "divine beauty."
   * Context: Labeling the glowing halo of light surrounding her head.
* अरुण (Aruṇa):
* Meaning: "The reddish glow of the rising sun" or "dawn."
   * Context: Used twice on the right side to compare her brilliant, life-giving light to the early morning sun touching the red leaves.

------------------------------
## PANEL 4 (Verse 12.4): The Triumphant Victory## The Main Phonetic Phrase

* जय जय हे... जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिने... (Jaya Jaya He...):
* Meaning: "Victory, victory to You! Victory to You, O Slayer of the demon Mahishasura!"
   * Context: The ultimate celebratory chorus of the stotram, hailing her victory over the chaotic ego.

## Individual Labels (Right to Left)

* शैलसुते (Śailasute):
* Meaning: "Daughter of the Mountain" (derived from Shaila = mountain, Suta = child/daughter).
   * Context: Addressing her divine origin as Parvati, born of the Himalayas.
* रम्यकपर्द्दिनि (Ramya-kapardini):
* Meaning: "One with beautiful, braided, or mat-locked hair."
   * Context: Praising her elegant, flowing tresses as she stands victorious.
* महिषासुर (Mahiṣāsura):
* Meaning: "The Buffalo Demon."
   * Context: Labeling the fallen demon king beneath her feet, symbolizing the final conquest over ignorance and egoism.

------------------------------
## The Big Picture Integration
When you put these sound cues together (Ranat → Lasat → Kanat), the stotram is purposefully leading your mind through a complete cycle:
$$\text{Metallic Battlefield Clanging (Ranat)} \longrightarrow \text{Gentle Forest Swaying (Lasat)} \longrightarrow \text{Blinding Divine Radiance (Kanat)}$$ 
It reminds the chanter that the ultimate cosmic power encompasses the raw noise of life's battles, the absolute innocence of nature, and the unshakeable inner light of the Self all at once.
