अयि गिरिनन्दिनि नन्दितमेदिनि विश्वविनोदिनि नन्दिनुते

1.1: Salutations to You O Divine Mother, I Invoke You; Who is the Daughter of the Mountain; By Whose presence the whole World is filled with Joy; For Whom the whole World is a Divine Play and Who is Praised by Nandi,

गिरिवरविन्ध्यशिरोऽधिनिवासिनि विष्णुविलासिनि जिष्णुनुते ।

1.2: (I Invoke You O Devi) Who, resides on the peak of the mountain range, Vindhya Mountains ; Who gives Joy to Lord Vishnu (as His sister) and Who is Praised by Lord Indra,

भगवति हे शितिकण्ठकुटुम्बिनि भूरिकुटुम्बिनि भूरिकृते

1.3: O Goddess Bhagavati, Who is the Consort of the One with Blue Throat (Lord Shiva); Who has Many Many Relations in this World (being the Cosmic Mother) and Who has Created Abundance (in Creation),

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १ ॥

1.4: Victory to You, Victory to You, (I take Refuge in Your Auspicious Feet) O the Destroyer of Demon Mahishasura; (Victory to You) Who Shine with Beautiful Locks of Hair and Who is the Daughter of the Mountain.

This verse invokes Maa Durga as the ultimate source of cosmic bliss and divine strength who dwells majestically on the summits of the Vindhya mountains alongside Shiva, Vishnu, Nandi and Indra. By slaying the buffalo-demon Mahishasura, She symbolizes the ultimate victory of divine intellect and spiritual awakening over human ego, laziness, and ignorance.

सुरवरवर्षिणि दुर्धरधर्षिणि दुर्मुखमर्षिणि हर्षरते

2.1: O Divine Mother Who Showers Boons on the Devas; Who Overpowered Demon Durdhara (Unrestrainable) and Endured Demon Durmukha (Foul-Mouthed) and finally slayed him, and Who Delight in Her own Bliss.

त्रिभुवनपोषिणि शङ्करतोषिणि किल्बिषमोषिणि घोषरते

2.2: Who Sustain and Nourish the Three-Worlds; Who Delight Lord Shankara by removing sins by Wiping away Deep-Seated Impurities; Who Delight in the Roar of Battle.

दनुजनिरोषिणि दितिसुतरोषिणि दुर्मदशोषिणि सिन्धुसुते

2.3: Who Quell the Wrath of the Danavas and is Angry with the Daityas ; Who Dry up the Foolish Pride of Demons; and Who is the Daughter of the Ocean.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ २ ॥

2.4: Victory to You, O Mother the Destroyer of Demon Mahishasura; Who Shines with Beautiful Locks of Hair and Who is the Daughter of the Mountain.

Six Demons: (kāma) is any kind of longing for sensory gratification,wealth, or power. Inherent in it is the sense of deficiency, limitation, or separation. Any desire is ultimately the desire for wholeness. When thwarted, it may turn to anger (krodha); paradoxically, when gratified it may become inflamed into greed (lobha). Because the ego, by its very nature, remains deficient, no amount of gratification can fill the void. Additionally, fixation on the ego manifests as pride (mada), a sense of superiority that in turn can engender jealousy (mātsarya). All these entangled passions thrive in delusion (moha), which clouds awareness and distorts self-perception.❤️

2

अयि जगदम्ब मदम्ब कदम्ब वनप्रियवासिनि हासरते

3.1: O Mother of the Universe, my very own Mother! Who resides intimately as my Mother; Who loves to dwell in the beautiful Kadamba forest, and Who delights in a joyful, radiant smile.

शिखरि शिरोमणि तुङ्गहिमलय शृङ्गनिजालय मध्यगते ।

3.2: Whose true home is midst the crest-jewel of mountains, resting upon the lofty, snow-capped peaks of the majestic Himalayas.

मधुमधुरे मधुकैटभगञ्जिनि कैटभभञ्जिनि रासरते

3.3: Who is sweeter than honey itself, yet turned into the fierce crusher of the demon Madhu, the shatterer of the demon Kaitabha, and Who delights in the divine cosmic dance.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ३ ॥

3.4: Victory, victory to You, O Destroyer of the monstrous demon Mahishasura; You who possess beautiful, intricately woven locks of hair, and Who are the Daughter of the Mountain.

Samatvam (समत्वम्): Equanimity: Like nature abiding in effortless balance, our true nature is peace. Attachment and Aversion, symbolized by Madhu and Kaitabha, arise from the play of the gunas and pull the mind into conflict and restlessness. When attachment and aversion arise, the mind becomes entangled in resistance and craving. Maa Durga's grace will dissolve these inner demons, helping us return to our original stillness and respond to every situation with equanimity and sweetness.

3

अयि शतखण्डविखण्डितरुण्डवितुण्डितशुण्डगजाधिपते ।

4.1: O Divine Mother who cuts the enemy into a 100 pieces , into tiny atoms ; demolishes the enemy to the depths of doom , and who destroys Shunda and his army of elephants .

रिपुगजगण्डविदारणचण्डपराक्रमशुण्डमृगाधिपते ॥

4.2: O Divine Mother who smashes the temples of the enemy’s elephants , who scatters the army of demon Chanda ; who displays mighty valour against demon Sunda , and whose lion‑vehicle strikes Munda’s forehead and destroys his elephant army .

निजभुजदण्डनिपातितखण्डविपातितमुण्डभटाधिपते ।

4.3: O Divine Mother who with Her own arms delivers punishment , who hurls endless divine weapons ; who wields a brilliant sword like lightning and destroys Chanda & Munda along with their generals .

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥

4.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautifully‑haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Vairāgya: Chanda Vritti (Ego-Driven Chase) and Munda Vritti (Ignorant/Reactive Withdrawal): Both happiness and sadness arising from the Chanda and Munda Vṛtti are temporary states of mind. They must be observed objectively with true detachment (Vairāgya), rather than avoided through reactive withdrawal.

4

अयि रणदुर्मद शत्रुवधोदित दुर्धरनिर्जर शक्तिभृते ।

5.1: O Divine Mother who is invincible and rises to slay the arrogant enemies with evil intentions , and who mercilessly eliminates evil forceswith her infinite strength .

चतुरविचार धुरीणमहाशिव दूतकृत प्रमथाधिपते ॥

5.2: O Divine Mother, who made Great Shiva—the chief of the Pramatha ganas and the foremost expert in clever diplomacy—Your messenger.

दुरितदुरीह दुराशयदुर्मति दानवदूत कृतान्तमते ।

5.3: O Divine Mother, who completely put an end to the evil designs, wicked motives, and malicious minds of the demon messengers.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ५ ॥

5.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Sakshi:In the context of mindfulness, this verse represents the shift from reaction to observation. The demon messengers are our ruminations and anxious thoughts. When we invoke our inner power (Shakti) and surrender the struggle, our higher consciousness (Shiva) handles the situation with stillness and diplomacy. It teaches us that not every intrusive thought requires a reaction. By stepping back, we allow a deeper cosmic intelligence to neutralize the mental noise, choosing restful detachment over impulsive action.

5

अयि शरणागत वैरिवधुवर वीरवराभय दायकरे ।

6.1: O Divine Mother, who grants refuge to those who surrender, and bestows fearlessness even upon the noble wives and heroic warriors of Your enemies when they seek Your protection.

त्रिभुवनमस्तक शूलविरोधि शिरोऽधिकृतामल शूलकरे ॥

6.2: O Divine Mother, whose power governs the three worlds, and who wields the pure trident that destroys all opposition to Divine Truth.

दुमिदुमितामर धुन्दुभिनादमहोमुखरीकृत दिङ्मकरे ।

6.3: O Divine Mother, whose victory is celebrated by the resounding celestial drums, filling all directions with divine sound and glory.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ६ ॥

6.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Dharmic principle: Divine power is always balanced by Divine compassion. Durga destroys harmful tendencies, but she offers protection to anyone who sincerely seeks refuge, even those associated with the opposing side. Spiritually, the verse teaches that once ego-driven tendencies are defeated, we should not continue fighting remnants of the past with bitterness. Instead, wisdom must be accompanied by compassion. The sound of the celestial drums symbolizes the harmony that emerges when justice and mercy work together.

6

अयि निजहुङ्कृति मात्रनिराकृत धूम्रविलोचन धूम्रशते ।

7.1: O Divine Mother, who by a mere sound of Your sacred roar destroyed the demon Dhumralochanainto hundred smoke particles.

समरविशोषित शोणितबीज समुद्भवशोणित बीजलते ॥

7.2: O Mother, who dried up the battlefield where Raktabīja and his countless blood-born duplicates arose

शिवशिवशुम्भ निशुम्भमहाहव तर्पितभूत पिशाचरते ।

7.3: O Divine One, who in the Great Auspicious Sacrifice (resembling a Yagna) of Shumbha and Nishumbha satisfied the thirst of Ghosts and Fiends (attending Lord Shiva), restoring cosmic balance through righteous battle.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ७ ॥

7.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Chitta Vritti Nirodha (निरोध):The demons are no longer external forces but symbolic of mental patterns: confusion (Dhumralochana) and self-replicating thoughts (Raktabīja). The Goddess does not fight them endlessly; instead, her vibration alone dissolves their power. This reflects the principle that awareness itself is transformative. When consciousness becomes steady and non-reactive, the compulsive multiplication of thoughts stops naturally without force.

7

धनुरनुषङ्ग रणक्षणसङ्ग परिस्फुरदङ्ग नटत्कटके

8.1: O Divine Mother, who is deeply engaged in battle at every moment with intense cosmic energy quivering through Your limbs, and whose bracelets appear to dance on Your vibrant arms following the swift, continuous stringing movements of Your bow.

कनकपिशङ्ग पृषत्कनिषङ्ग रसद्भटशृङ्ग हताबटुके ।

8.2: O Divine Mother, whose golden arrows shine with a reddish hue as they are launched from Your brilliant quiver packed with arrows, smiting down the vain pride of roaring enemy warriors and completely destroying them in battle.

कृतचतुरङ्ग बलक्षितिरङ्ग घटद्बहुरङ्ग रटद्बटुके

8.3: O Divine Mother, who faces the fourfold traditional army of the enemy to break their strength on the playground of the battlefield, creating an expansive drama of diverse divine plays where Your shouting spirit-clans and young attendants echo Your glory.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ८ ॥

8.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Sthitaprajna (स्थितप्रज्ञ):When the daily Chaturanga of life attacks - 1. Tune into your inner Shakti during stressful moments, shift from panicked reaction to centered action by anchoring your mind and body. , 2. Practice the Chandi Breath—inhaling deep strength and exhaling tension with a powerful, clearing "Haa" release.3. Maintain the poise of the Dancing Bracelet by consciously softening your jaw and shoulders with an upright posture. 4. Mentally step back to view your stressors as an external army rather than your identity, repeating the grounding mantra, "I am the weapon, She is the warrior".
