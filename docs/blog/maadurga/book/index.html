अयि गिरिनन्दिनि नन्दितमेदिनि विश्वविनोदिनि नन्दिनुते

1.1: Salutations to You O Divine Mother, I Invoke You; Who is the Daughter of the Mountain; By Whose presence the whole World is filled with Joy; For Whom the whole World is a Divine Play and Who is Praised by Nandi,

गिरिवरविन्ध्यशिरोऽधिनिवासिनि विष्णुविलासिनि जिष्णुनुते ।

1.2: (I Invoke You O Devi) Who, resides on the peak of the mountain range, Vindhya Mountains ; Who gives Joy to Lord Vishnu (as His sister) and Who is Praised by Lord Indra,

भगवति हे शितिकण्ठकुटुम्बिनि भूरिकुटुम्बिनि भूरिकृते

1.3: O Goddess Bhagavati, Who is the Consort of the One with Blue Throat (Lord Shiva); Who has Many Many Relations in this World (being the Cosmic Mother) and Who has Created Abundance (in Creation),

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १ ॥

1.4: Victory to You, Victory to You, (I take Refuge in Your Auspicious Feet) O the Destroyer of Demon Mahishasura; (Victory to You) Who Shine with Beautiful Locks of Hair and Who is the Daughter of the Mountain.

This verse invokes Maa Durga as the ultimate source of cosmic bliss and divine strength who dwells majestically on the summits of the Vindhya mountains alongside Shiva, Vishnu, Nandi and Indra. By slaying the buffalo-demon Mahishasura, She symbolizes the ultimate victory of divine intellect and spiritual awakening over human ego, laziness, and ignorance.

1

सुरवरवर्षिणि दुर्धरधर्षिणि दुर्मुखमर्षिणि हर्षरते

2.1: O Divine Mother Who Showers Boons on the Devas; Who Overpowered Demon Durdhara (Unrestrainable) and Endured Demon Durmukha (Foul-Mouthed) and finally slayed him, and Who Delight in Her own Bliss.

त्रिभुवनपोषिणि शङ्करतोषिणि किल्बिषमोषिणि घोषरते

2.2: Who Sustain and Nourish the Three-Worlds; Who Delight Lord Shankara by removing sins by Wiping away Deep-Seated Impurities; Who Delight in the Roar of Battle.

दनुजनिरोषिणि दितिसुतरोषिणि दुर्मदशोषिणि सिन्धुसुते

2.3: Who Quell the Wrath of the Danavas and is Angry with the Daityas ; Who Dry up the Foolish Pride of Demons; and Who is the Daughter of the Ocean.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ २ ॥

2.4: Victory to You, O Mother the Destroyer of Demon Mahishasura; Who Shines with Beautiful Locks of Hair and Who is the Daughter of the Mountain.

Six Demons: (kāma) is any kind of longing for sensory gratification,wealth, or power. Inherent in it is the sense of deficiency, limitation, or separation. Any desire is ultimately the desire for wholeness. When thwarted, it may turn to anger (krodha); paradoxically, when gratified it may become inflamed into greed (lobha). Because the ego, by its very nature, remains deficient, no amount of gratification can fill the void. Additionally, fixation on the ego manifests as pride (mada), a sense of superiority that in turn can engender jealousy (mātsarya). All these entangled passions thrive in delusion (moha), which clouds awareness and distorts self-perception.❤️

2

अयि जगदम्ब मदम्ब कदम्ब वनप्रियवासिनि हासरते

3.1: O Mother of the Universe, my very own Mother! Who resides intimately as my Mother; Who loves to dwell in the beautiful Kadamba forest, and Who delights in a joyful, radiant smile.

शिखरि शिरोमणि तुङ्गहिमलय शृङ्गनिजालय मध्यगते ।

3.2: Whose true home is midst the crest-jewel of mountains, resting upon the lofty, snow-capped peaks of the majestic Himalayas.

मधुमधुरे मधुकैटभगञ्जिनि कैटभभञ्जिनि रासरते

3.3: Who is sweeter than honey itself, yet turned into the fierce crusher of the demon Madhu, the shatterer of the demon Kaitabha, and Who delights in the divine cosmic dance.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ३ ॥

3.4: Victory, victory to You, O Destroyer of the monstrous demon Mahishasura; You who possess beautiful, intricately woven locks of hair, and Who are the Daughter of the Mountain.

Samatvam (समत्वम्): Equanimity: Like nature abiding in effortless balance, our true nature is peace. Attachment and Aversion, symbolized by Madhu and Kaitabha, arise from the play of the gunas and pull the mind into conflict and restlessness. When attachment and aversion arise, the mind becomes entangled in resistance and craving. Maa Durga's grace will dissolve these inner demons, helping us return to our original stillness and respond to every situation with equanimity and sweetness.

3

अयि शतखण्डविखण्डितरुण्डवितुण्डितशुण्डगजाधिपते ।

4.1: O Divine Mother who cuts the enemy into a 100 pieces , into tiny atoms ; demolishes the enemy to the depths of doom , and who destroys Shunda and his army of elephants .

रिपुगजगण्डविदारणचण्डपराक्रमशुण्डमृगाधिपते ॥

4.2: O Divine Mother who smashes the temples of the enemy’s elephants , who scatters the army of demon Chanda ; who displays mighty valour against demon Sunda , and whose lion‑vehicle strikes Munda’s forehead and destroys his elephant army .

निजभुजदण्डनिपातितखण्डविपातितमुण्डभटाधिपते ।

4.3: O Divine Mother who with Her own arms delivers punishment , who hurls endless divine weapons ; who wields a brilliant sword like lightning and destroys Chanda & Munda along with their generals .

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥

4.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautifully‑haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Vairāgya: Chanda Vritti (Ego-Driven Chase) and Munda Vritti (Ignorant/Reactive Withdrawal): Both happiness and sadness arising from the Chanda and Munda Vṛtti are temporary states of mind. They must be observed objectively with true detachment (Vairāgya), rather than avoided through reactive withdrawal.

4

अयि रणदुर्मद शत्रुवधोदित दुर्धरनिर्जर शक्तिभृते ।

5.1: O Divine Mother who is invincible and rises to slay the arrogant enemies with evil intentions , and who mercilessly eliminates evil forceswith her infinite strength .

चतुरविचार धुरीणमहाशिव दूतकृत प्रमथाधिपते ॥

5.2: O Divine Mother, who made Great Shiva—the chief of the Pramatha ganas and the foremost expert in clever diplomacy—Your messenger.

दुरितदुरीह दुराशयदुर्मति दानवदूत कृतान्तमते ।

5.3: O Divine Mother, who completely put an end to the evil designs, wicked motives, and malicious minds of the demon messengers.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ५ ॥

5.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Sakshi:In the context of mindfulness, this verse represents the shift from reaction to observation. The demon messengers are our ruminations and anxious thoughts. When we invoke our inner power (Shakti) and surrender the struggle, our higher consciousness (Shiva) handles the situation with stillness and diplomacy. It teaches us that not every intrusive thought requires a reaction. By stepping back, we allow a deeper cosmic intelligence to neutralize the mental noise, choosing restful detachment over impulsive action.

5

अयि शरणागत वैरिवधुवर वीरवराभय दायकरे ।

6.1: O Divine Mother, who grants refuge to those who surrender, and bestows fearlessness even upon the noble wives and heroic warriors of Your enemies when they seek Your protection.

त्रिभुवनमस्तक शूलविरोधि शिरोऽधिकृतामल शूलकरे ॥

6.2: O Divine Mother, whose power governs the three worlds, and who wields the pure trident that destroys all opposition to Divine Truth.

दुमिदुमितामर धुन्दुभिनादमहोमुखरीकृत दिङ्मकरे ।

6.3: O Divine Mother, whose victory is celebrated by the resounding celestial drums, filling all directions with divine sound and glory.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ६ ॥

6.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Dharmic principle: Divine power is always balanced by Divine compassion. Durga destroys harmful tendencies, but she offers protection to anyone who sincerely seeks refuge, even those associated with the opposing side. Spiritually, the verse teaches that once ego-driven tendencies are defeated, we should not continue fighting remnants of the past with bitterness. Instead, wisdom must be accompanied by compassion. The sound of the celestial drums symbolizes the harmony that emerges when justice and mercy work together.

6

अयि निजहुङ्कृति मात्रनिराकृत धूम्रविलोचन धूम्रशते ।

7.1: O Divine Mother, who by a mere sound of Your sacred roar destroyed the demon Dhumralochanainto hundred smoke particles.

समरविशोषित शोणितबीज समुद्भवशोणित बीजलते ॥

7.2: O Mother, who dried up the battlefield where Raktabīja and his countless blood-born duplicates arose

शिवशिवशुम्भ निशुम्भमहाहव तर्पितभूत पिशाचरते ।

7.3: O Divine One, who in the Great Auspicious Sacrifice (resembling a Yagna) of Shumbha and Nishumbha satisfied the thirst of Ghosts and Fiends (attending Lord Shiva), restoring cosmic balance through righteous battle.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ७ ॥

7.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Chitta Vritti Nirodha (निरोध):The demons are no longer external forces but symbolic of mental patterns: confusion (Dhumralochana) and self-replicating thoughts (Raktabīja). The Goddess does not fight them endlessly; instead, her vibration alone dissolves their power. This reflects the principle that awareness itself is transformative. When consciousness becomes steady and non-reactive, the compulsive multiplication of thoughts stops naturally without force.

7

धनुरनुषङ्ग रणक्षणसङ्ग परिस्फुरदङ्ग नटत्कटके

8.1: O Divine Mother, who is deeply engaged in battle at every moment with intense cosmic energy quivering through Your limbs, and whose bracelets appear to dance on Your vibrant arms following the swift, continuous stringing movements of Your bow.

कनकपिशङ्ग पृषत्कनिषङ्ग रसद्भटशृङ्ग हताबटुके ।

8.2: O Divine Mother, whose golden arrows shine with a reddish hue as they are launched from Your brilliant quiver packed with arrows, smiting down the vain pride of roaring enemy warriors and completely destroying them in battle.

कृतचतुरङ्ग बलक्षितिरङ्ग घटद्बहुरङ्ग रटद्बटुके

8.3: O Divine Mother, who faces the fourfold traditional army of the enemy to break their strength on the playground of the battlefield, creating an expansive drama of diverse divine plays where Your shouting spirit-clans and young attendants echo Your glory.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ८ ॥

8.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Sthitaprajna (स्थितप्रज्ञ):When the daily Chaturanga of life attacks - 1. Tune into your inner Shakti during stressful moments, shift from panicked reaction to centered action by anchoring your mind and body. , 2. Practice the Chandi Breath—inhaling deep strength and exhaling tension with a powerful, clearing "Haa" release.3. Maintain the poise of the Dancing Bracelet by consciously softening your jaw and shoulders with an upright posture. 4. Mentally step back to view your stressors as an external army rather than your identity, repeating the grounding mantra, "I am the weapon, She is the warrior".

8

सुरललना ततथेयि तथेयि कृताभिनयोदर नृत्यरते

9.1: Who Delights in Dancing, following the rhythmic footwork of "Ta-Tha-Theyi, Ta-Theyi" accompanied by the dramatic gestures and expressions performed by the Divine Celestial Maidens (Apsaras),

कृतकुकुथः कुकुथो गडदादिकताल कुतूहल गानरते ।

9.2: (I Invoke You O Devi) Who Delights in the Music filled with eager curiosity, set to complex beats like "Kukutha, Kukutha" and various rhythmic cycles starting with "Gaddada",

धुधुकुट धुक्कुट धिंधिमित ध्वनि धीर मृदंग निनादरते

9.3: Who Delights in the deep reverberation of the solemn Mridangam Drum, which produces the powerful and majestic rhythmic sounds of "Dhu-Dhu-Kuta, Dhuk-Kuta" and "Dhim-Dhimi",

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ९ ॥

9.4: Victory to You, Victory to You, (I take Refuge in Your Auspicious Feet) O the Destroyer of Demon Mahishasura; (Victory to You) Who Shine with Beautiful Locks of Hair and Who is the Daughter of the Mountain.

This verse symbolizes the cosmic dance of divine energy, where music, rhythm, and artistic expression celebrate the triumph of the Goddess over ignorance and disorder. It presents Durga not only as a warrior but as the source of harmony, beauty, creativity, and the rhythmic order of the universe itself.

9

जय जय जप्य जयेजयशब्द परस्तुति तत्परविश्वनुते

10.1: Victory to You, victory to You, O Mother whose name is worthy of constant meditation, whose praises are sung with cries of 'Victory! Victory!', and who is adored and worshipped by the entire universe and who chant your supreme praise as hymns.

झणझणझिञ्झिमि झिङ्कृत नूपुरशिञ्जितमोहित भूतपते ।

10.2: The enchanting sound of Your anklets, ringing with musical jingling tones, captivates even Lord Shiva, the Lord of the Ghosts and Goblins.

नटित नटार्ध नटी नट नायक नाटितनाट्य सुगानरते

10.3: You delight in the divine dance, performing gracefully with the Lord whose half is the Divine Dancer (Shiva), rejoicing as the supreme dancer, the leader of all dancers, and the lover of sacred dance and melodious music.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १० ॥

10.4: Victory, victory to You, the Slayer of Mahishasura, the Beautiful One with lovely tresses, and the Daughter of the Mountain. May Your triumph be eternal.

The performance serves as a visual metaphor for the non-dual nature of ultimate reality (Advaita). While human perception breaks the world down into binaries—good and bad, male and female, light and dark—the Ardhanarishwara dance shows that these are merely two sides of the same single coin.

10

अयि सुमनःसुमनःसुमनःसुमनःसुमनोहरकान्तियुते

11.1: O Goddess whose beautiful mind is united with a charming and captivating appearance (radiance).

श्रितरजनिरजनिरजनिरजनिरजनिकरवक्त्रवृते ।

11.2: Whose beautiful face puts to shame the cool, soothing beauty of the moon (which shines at night).

सुनयनविभ्रमरभ्रमरभ्रमरभ्रमरभ्रमराधिपते

11.3:Whose beautiful, moving eyes conquer the dark, buzzing beauty of swarms of bees .

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ११॥

11.4: Victory, victory to You, the Slayer of Mahishasura, the Beautiful One with lovely tresses, and the Daughter of the Mountain.

Waxing Moon (Shukla Paksha) → growth, energy increase, festivals like Navratri, devotional expansion.

Waning Moon (Krishna Paksha) → reflection, release, inward spiritual work

The Moon is the smiling face of Maa Durga who grants us grace and peace. When we are gazing at the Moon, we are contacting Her presence and can draw in her subtle energies.

11

सहितमहाहव मल्लमतल्लिक मल्लितरल्लक मल्लरते

12.1: O Divine Goddess engaged in a fierce cosmic battle, accompanied by a great war, who fights against the best of wrestlers and agile, fierce warriors, and who delights in subduing such mighty opponents.

विरचितवल्लिक पल्लिकमल्लिक झिल्लिकभिल्लिक वर्गवृते ।

12.2: You are beautifully adorned with creeping vines, dwelling near small forest hamlets, surrounded by wild jasmine flowers and the humming of crickets, and ever encircled by the tribal communities of forest dwellers.

शितकृतफुल्ल समुल्लसितारुण तल्लजपल्लव सल्ललिते

12.3: Your divine form is charmingly graceful, radiant like highly polished and sharpened weapons, blooming beautifully like a fully blossomed flower, and splendidly shining with the reddish glow of the rising sun on excellent tender leaves.

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १२ ॥

12.4: Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of the demon Mahishasura, the beautiful-haired Daughter of the Mountain!

This illustrates that both courage and innocence can stay together. It is not necessary that when we engage in the battles of life, we forget to realize the beauty and innocence around us and within us. The forest dwellers and wild flowers symbolize the natural state of our true Self, which needs to invoke Durga at the time of a fight.

12

अविरलगण्ड गलन्मदमेदुर मत्तमतङ्ग जराजपते

13.1: O Divine Mother, who is like a Sovereign King of Intoxicated Elephants, from whose continuous temples (cheeks) the thick, intoxicating rut-fluid oozes out and streams down incessantly (manifesting as a flood of arts, beauty, and cosmic power).

त्रिभुवनभुषण भूतकलानिधि रूपपयोनिधि राजसुते ।

13.2: O Divine Mother, You are the supreme ornament of the three worlds, the infinite treasure-house of all arts and creation, an unfathomable ocean of beauty and grace, and the divine Daughter of the Mountain King!

अयि सुदतीजन लालसमानस मोहन मन्मथराजसुते

13.3: O Divine Mother, You are the enchanting forcedeeply yearning minds of the beautifully smiling maidens, and You are the Sovereign Daughter who rules over the spiritual realm of pure love and desire (surpassing even Manmatha, the king of attraction)!

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १३ ॥

13.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

When one has understood theoretically and fights the vasanas constantly without feeling shame or guilt (as theory is understood but in practice it disappears), eventually one can slowly remove the kleshas and surrender to Durga—the Pure Awareness. Having reached this stage of pure awareness, one becomes a source of pure energy that is represented by arts and beauty. All beautiful, innocent maidens, symbolizing purity, are deeply attracted to this energy. This means one is not just in bliss, but becomes a source of bliss by just the positive energy radiating from within.

13

कमलदलामल कोमलकान्ति कलाकलितामल भाललते

14.1: O Divine Mother, on Your elegant, creeper-like forehead shines a tender, soft radiance, which is spotless and pure like a flawless lotus petal , and beautifully adorned with the curved crescent moon.

सकलविलास कलानिलयक्रम केलिचलत्कल हंसकुले ।

14.2: O Divine Mother, You are the ultimate abode of all divine grace and creative arts, and Your graceful, rhythmic movements play out like the beautifully ordered steps of a flock of majestic royal swans!

अलिकुलसङ्कुल कुवलयमण्डल मौलिमिलद्बकुलालिकुले

14.3: O Divine Mother, Your hair is adorned with clusters of blue lotuses swarming with buzzing bumblebees, which elegantly intertwine upon the crown of Your head with fragrant Bakula flowers, blending their swarms together!

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १४ ॥

14.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Demons are not external; they are what we create internally using tools like Ego, Prejudice, Desires, Pride, and our biological and psychological conditioning. In a way, these demons are like a mirage—on close inspection, they do not truly exist. Focusing on Maa Durga turns your awareness inward and upward toward the Ajna Chakra instead of chasing the mirage, which is a completely futile effort. However, to be able to steadily meditate on Her and hold this space, you need Her divine Grace combined with unshakeable Perseverance.

14

करमुरलीरव वीजितकूजित लज्जितकोकिल मञ्जुमते

15.1: O Divine Mother, You are endowed with a beautifully sweet and charming mind; the melody from the flute in Your hands vibrates and flows out with such exquisite cooing notes that it puts even the sweetest nightingale to total shame!

मिलितपुलिन्द मनोहरगुञ्जित रञ्जितशैल निकुञ्जगते ।

15.2: O Divine Mother, You are gathered together and mingling with the simple forest tribes, and You gracefully move through the mountain groves which are deeply resonated with joy, echoing with their beautiful, heart-captivating humming and singing!

निजगणभूत महाशबरीगण सद्गुणसम्भृत केलितले

15.3: O Divine Mother, You awaken Your own innate spiritual virtues within the throngs of simple, raw tribal forest women, endowing them with excellent and noble traits as You participate in their vast expanse of playful, divine sports!

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १५ ॥

15.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

When we are observing a klesha in someone, pause and observe if the Lake(Mind) is muddy ,clear your own klesha through introspection first. When the Lake of the Mind Is Clear, It Reflects the Divine Within; When the Flute Is Free of Vasanas, It Sings the Music of the Soul.Grace of Maa Durga and deep meditation, will make us a perfect flute—empty and unblocked. This allows the divine consciousness to pass through you effortlessly and emit the most beautiful music, putting even the sweetest cuckoos to total shame.

कटितटपीत दुकूलविचित्र मयुखतिरस्कृत चन्द्ररुचे

16.1: O Divine Mother, You are wrapped around Your waist in a fine yellow silk sari which is wondrously and intricately embroidered; the brilliant blazing rays of light radiating from it completely eclipse and throw into the shade the pristine luster and beauty of the moon!

प्रणतसुरासुर मौलिमणिस्फुर दंशुलसन्नख चन्द्ररुचे

16.2: O Divine Mother, both gods and demons bow down in deep reverence before You; the gems of their crowns flash and reflect vivid rays of light upon the beautifully shining nails of Your feet, which radiate like pristine crescent moons!

जितकनकाचल मौलिमदोर्जित निर्भरकुञ्जर कुम्भकुचे

16.3: O Divine Mother, Your majestic form surpasses the grandeur of the golden peaks of Mount Meru,and You overflow with a mighty, unrestrained vital force, radiating the ultimate abundance and unshakeable power of a majestic royal elephant!

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १६ ॥

16.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Any Yin and Yang, be it good and bad, success and failure, or happiness and sadness, we as humans obviously strive for one and fear, hate, or avoid the other. It is this very resistance which causes us to enjoy the good only briefly, while for the majority of our lives we are actually resisting or ruminating over the other. Pursue Artha and Kama strictly as per Dharma, but surrender the fruits—whether they bring good or bad, sickness or health—at the feet of the Mother. She is the ultimate source of both; it is all Her Maya. Transcend and develop equanimity. Then, that surrender will reflect a calm, cool, soothing moonlight from the Mother's toenails, surpassing the absolute best of joys in this universe. This realization allows one to walk through life like a majestic elephant, rooted in our true nature of “I AM,” solid as the golden Meru Mountain, leading us eventually to our ultimate destination: Moksha

विजितसहस्रकरैक सहस्रकरैक सहस्रकरैकनुते

17.1: O Divine Mother, Your incomparable power and splendor surpass and triumph over even those of the mightiest thousand-armed celestial beings, while You are reverently praised and worshiped by innumerable devotees their thousand hands raised in devotion.!

कृतसुरतारक सङ्गरतारक सङ्गरतारक सूनुसुते ।

17.2: O Divine Mother, You rescued the gods from the terror of the demon Tarakasura, You are the ultimate savior who delivers victory in the battles of life, and You are the Mother of that glorious divine warrior son (Lord Kartikeya)! who destroyed Tarakasura

सुरथसमाधि समानसमाधि समाधिसमाधि सुजातरते ।

17.3: O Divine Mother, You are the One who forcefully and intensely elevated both King Suratha (the distressed ruler) and Samadhi the merchant (the broken-hearted seeker) into the exact same ultimate state of unshakeable equanimity and grace!

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १७ ॥

17.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .

Have razor-sharp spiritual wisdom that cuts cleanly through ignorance. Conquer, tame, and ultimately ride your own pride, vanity, and reactive desires rather than being ruled by them. Stop demanding that the external world remain perfectly ordered, pure, or aligned with your preferences. Instead, seek the ultimate Truth and become firmly established in **Sama**—perfect equanimity. This radical surrender of personal preferences does not weaken you; it makes you truly free. Established in unwavering equanimity, you become the sovereign of your own mind, no longer dependent on external circumstances for peace or fulfillment. Whether faced with gain or loss, praise or blame, pleasure or pain, you remain unshaken, resting in your true nature. Such abiding Self-realization is freedom itself. It dissolves the bondage of karma and the cycle of birth and death, revealing **Moksha** not as a distant goal but as a living reality. This is **Jīvanmukti**—liberation while still living.

पदकमलं करुणानिलये वरिवस्यति योऽनुदिनं सुशिवे

18.1: O Divine Mother, abode of boundless compassion, O supremely auspicious One, whoever lovingly worships Your lotus feet day after day,

अयि कमले कमलानिलये कमलानिलयः स कथं न भवेत् ।

18.2: O Divine Mother, Lotus-like One, whose abode is the sacred lotus, how could one who takes refuge in You not himself become a lotus-abode—a dwelling place of purity, beauty, and auspiciousness?

तव पदमेव परम्पदमित्यनुशीलयतो मम किं न शिवे

18.3: O Divine Mother, Your sacred feet alone are the Supreme Goal and ultimate state of liberation; for me who constantly contemplates and cultivates this truth, O Auspicious One, what indeed could remain unattainable?

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १८ ॥

18.4: O Divine Mother, Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura, O Beautiful-haired One, O Daughter of the Mountain!

Now that the devotee, through daily worship and unwavering devotion, has totally surrendered at the lotus feet of Maa Durga, the journey with the Goddess has brought about a profound realization. Through Her boundless, infinite compassion, the Mother has permanently transformed the inner world of the devotee into aKamalānilaya—a pristine Lotus Abode which is the supreme state of living Liberation. A lotus dynamically blooms in the middle of a messy, muddy pond, yet it stays completely afloat, pristine, and absolutely untouched by the surrounding mud, remaining so extraordinarily beautiful. In the exact same way, the seeker remains beautifully anchored in the world, yet entirely unaffected by its passing storms and friction.

मनोवैज्ञानिकशब्दकोश (Manovaijñānika-śabdakośa)

Sanskrit WordMeaning
इन्द्रIndra is the Ruler with all six gunas and troubled by the demons, and Durga is the Shakthi who saves him
ब्रह्माThe creative impetus and generative mind; linked to Durga as the cosmic architect whose prayers (Brahmadi Stuti) awaken her from Yoganidra, symbolizing the intellect calling upon divine power to initiate action against mental chaos
विष्णुVishnumaya (The Divine Illusion): The world of conditioned experience, shaped by the three guṇas, is difficult to rise above. Yet those who wholeheartedly take refuge in the Divine can transcend its binding influence. In many Hindu traditions, Durga is understood as the Divine power that both governs the material world and guides spiritual seekers. Through life's experiences—including challenges that cultivate wisdom and detachment—she helps individuals progress toward a deeper realization of the Divine.
शिवThe unmoving, pure background consciousness; linked to Durga as her eternal consort, where Shiva is the silent witness and Durga is the active, dynamic energy (Shakti) that manifests and fights the mental battles
नन्दिThe disciplined, unwavering mind; linked to Durga as Shiva’s vehicle and commander of his forces, symbolizing absolute devotion and instinctual energy channeled in service of the divine battle against the ego
श्रद्धाA trusting confidence that motivates commitment, practice, and perseverance even before one has direct personal realization.
प्रारब्धThis is the specific portion of your vast storehouse of past karma (Sanchita Karma) that has matured and is allotted to be experienced in your current life duration. It manifests as your unalterable destiny—such as your physical body, birth environment, parents, and major life events that are beyond your control. Your Prarabdha dictates the life situations and events you must face today.
संस्कारHow you react to those life situations creates fresh mental impressions, known as Samskaras.These are the deep, subtle psychological imprints left behind in the subconscious mind by our past experiences, thoughts, actions, and emotions. Every action you perform leaves a "groove" or trace in your consciousness.
वासनाOver time, accumulated Samskaras cluster together to form Vasanas (your default psychological habits and desires).
अर्धनारीश्वरA symbol of the Samkhya philosophy which talks of Purusha (the male energy) and Prakriti (the female energy) together makes the cosmic energy.
