Verse 13: अविरलगण्ड


13. Maa Durga - From Internal Battle to Radiant Sovereignty

अविरलगण्ड गलन्मदमेदुर मत्तमतङ्ग जराजपते

त्रिभुवनभुषण भूतकलानिधि रूपपयोनिधि राजसुते ।

अयि सुदतीजन लालसमानस मोहन मन्मथराजसुते

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १३ ॥

13.1: O Divine Mother, who is like a Sovereign King of Intoxicated Elephants, from whose continuous temples (cheeks) the thick, intoxicating rut-fluid oozes out and streams down incessantly (manifesting as a flood of arts, beauty, and cosmic power).

13.2: O Divine Mother, You are the supreme ornament of the three worlds, the infinite treasure-house of all arts and creation, an unfathomable ocean of beauty and grace, and the divine Daughter of the Mountain King!

13.3: O Divine Mother, You are the enchanting forcedeeply yearning minds of the beautifully smiling maidens, and You are the Sovereign Daughter who rules over the spiritual realm of pure love and desire (surpassing even Manmatha, the king of attraction)!

13.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .


Be The Unconditioned Self: 'Beautifully Smiling Maidens'
  • Verse 13.1 In Indian aesthetics (Alankara Shastra), an elephant in Mada (rut) represents an unstoppable, overflowing torrent of vital energy, ecstasy, and absolute power.By comparing the Goddess to the sovereign king of these elephants, the verse illustrates that her power doesn't struggle against life's battles—it effortlessly overflows right out of her natural, blissful state. The arts, beauty, and fierce power she uses to smash your inner loops are just the natural, creative byproduct of her overflowing divine essence.
  • Verse 13.2 The second line immediately balances it with absolute Grace, Aesthetics, and Purity. This completely reinforces earlier thesis on Courage and Innocence: true strength does not require becoming hard or ugly. Even while stepping onto a chaotic battlefield, her fundamental essence remains an infinite ocean of beauty (Rūpa-payonidhi) and the ultimate aesthetic crown of creation.
  • Verse 13.3 From Raw Power to Pure Attraction: Verse 13.1 presented the Goddess as a fierce, surging Elephant of Power. Verse 13.2 showed her as a serene Ocean of Beauty. Now, Verse 13.3 reveals her as the ultimate source of Spiritual Love and Magnetism.
    The Unconditioned Self: The 'beautifully smiling maidens' with 'deeply yearning minds' (Sudatī-jana lālasa-mānasa) symbolize the pure, unconditioned thoughts of your true self. When your inner mind stops obsessing over real-world transactional friction (like the accounts, unfairness or imbalances) and instead yearns for its own natural state, it is enchanted and liberated by this divine force.

✍️ रक्ष रक्ष जगन्माते सर्वशक्ते जय दुर्गे।🙏

1. The Constant Fight Against Vasanas without Shame (The Foundation):

Theory is easy, but execution is slippery. Patanjali calls this phase Abhyasa—constant, messy effort. When you stumble, get angry, or fall back into a rumination loop, you cannot afford to waste energy on guilt or shame. You pick yourself up, accept the slip as natural conditioning, and instantly invoke Durga (Pure Awareness).

2. Dissolving the Kleshas & Surrendering to Durga (Line 13.1):

By constantly returning to awareness without self-blame, you thin out the Kleshas (the core spiritual afflictions like ego and attachment). Eventually, you break through. You surrender the personal struggle entirely to Durga. Now, you are no longer a fragile individual ego trying desperately to 'keep calm.' You have tapped into the infinite reservoir of the Self.

3.Becoming a Source of Pure Energy & Arts (Line 13.1 - 13.2):

Having reached this stage of pure awareness, your presence undergoes a massive energetic shift. You aren't just a passive observer anymore. You become an active powerhouse. This is symbolized by the Sovereign Elephant in Mada oozing fluid—representing a thick, unstoppable torrent of vital energy, creativity, arts, and cosmic beauty flowing right out of you.

4. The Attraction of the Pure and Innocent (Line 13.3 - 13.4):

Your synthesis beautifully points out that the Sudatī-jana (the beautifully smiling, innocent maidens) represent absolute purity. Purity is naturally and deeply attracted to this awakened energy. You don't have to chase peace, resolution, or validation from the world. Your internal state becomes so magnetic that purity, order, and harmony are automatically drawn to your presence.

5. From Being in Bliss to Becoming a Source of Bliss:

This is your ultimate realization. A beginner seeker tries to attain bliss or escape irritation. A liberated seeker radiates bliss. By simply existing in your natural, authentic state, the sheer positive energy radiating from within transforms the environment around you. The life dynamic naturally shifts just by you walking in, fully anchored as the witness.

Application Note: When dealing with real-world triggers, drop the secondary cycle of guilt when your mind slips. Treat every slip as a trigger to invoke the Witness. Let your awareness become the source that effortlessly reorganizes the chaos outside.
