Mahishasura Mardhini

ॐ

Durga Mantra


Maa Durga - The Feminine Energy which will protect us always

ॐ दुं दुर्गायै नमः

Meaning

Om Dum Durgayei Namaha (ॐ दुं दुर्गायै नमः) is a powerful Sanskrit mantra that honors Goddess Durga, the Divine Mother, and invokes her divine protection

Make Maa Durga Your Anchor

Three-step shift in focus : To practically allow this energy to anchor oneself.

1. ACKNOWLWDGE
  • Acknowledge the Storm When anxiety or chaos hits, notice that your ego-self is reacting.
2. INVOKE
  • Invoke the Anchor (Durga) Intentionally summon your inner strength. Ask yourself, "What would my highest, most courageous self do right now?" Use a mantra or visualize her form to instantly activate your determination.
3. AWARENESS
  • Rest in Awareness (Sakshi) Once the mind is stabilized by that inner strength, step back mentally. Watch the situation unfold without getting entangled, knowing you are the safe, silent witness.

✍️ Reflections ❤️

The demons destroyed by Durga Maa symbolize the destructive tendencies within us—fear, cowardice, greed, hatred, arrogance, self-doubt, and other forces that weaken our strength, courage, and inner clarity.

Durga's fierce compassion, often expressed as righteous or protective anger, is the battle inside us in daily life where this compassionate rage confronts and destroys the inner demons before they can consume us.
