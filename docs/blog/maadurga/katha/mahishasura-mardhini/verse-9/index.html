Mahishasura Mardhini

ॐ

Verse 9: सुरललना ततथेयि तथेयि


9. Maa Durga - The Cosmic Celebration of Divine Energy

सुरललना ततथेयि तथेयि कृताभिनयोदर नृत्यरते

कृतकुकुथः कुकुथो गडदादिकताल कुतूहल गानरते ।

धुधुकुट धुक्कुट धिंधिमित ध्वनि धीर मृदंग निनादरते

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ९ ॥

9.1: Who Delights in Dancing, following the rhythmic footwork of "Ta-Tha-Theyi, Ta-Theyi" accompanied by the dramatic gestures and expressions performed by the Divine Celestial Maidens (Apsaras),

9.2: (I Invoke You O Devi) Who Delights in the Music filled with eager curiosity, set to complex beats like "Kukutha, Kukutha" and various rhythmic cycles starting with "Gaddada",

9.3: Who Delights in the deep reverberation of the solemn Mridangam Drum, which produces the powerful and majestic rhythmic sounds of "Dhu-Dhu-Kuta, Dhuk-Kuta" and "Dhim-Dhimi",

9.4: Victory to You, Victory to You, (I take Refuge in Your Auspicious Feet) O the Destroyer of Demon Mahishasura; (Victory to You) Who Shine with Beautiful Locks of Hair and Who is the Daughter of the Mountain.


✍️ रक्ष रक्ष जगन्माते सर्वशक्ते जय दुर्गे।🙏

The verse highlights the Hindu idea that strength and beauty, power and compassion, can coexist in the same divine reality.

And suggests that after the destruction of ignorance, ego, and destructive tendencies (represented by Mahishasura), the natural state is celebration, harmony, and beauty.
