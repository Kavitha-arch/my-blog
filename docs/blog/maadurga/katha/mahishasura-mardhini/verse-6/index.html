Verse 6: अयि शरणागत


6. The Compassion of Durga Toward Those Who Surrender

अयि शरणागत वैरिवधुवर वीरवराभय दायकरे ।

त्रिभुवनमस्तक शूलविरोधि शिरोऽधिकृतामल शूलकरे ॥

दुमिदुमितामर धुन्दुभिनादमहोमुखरीकृत दिङ्मकरे ।

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ ६ ॥

6.1: O Divine Mother, who grants refuge to those who surrender, and bestows fearlessness even upon the noble wives and heroic warriors of Your enemies when they seek Your protection.

6.2: O Divine Mother, whose power governs the three worlds, and who wields the pure trident that destroys all opposition to Divine Truth.

6.3: O Divine Mother, whose victory is celebrated by the resounding celestial drums, filling all directions with divine sound and glory.

6.4: O Divine Mother Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of Mahishasura , O Beautiful-haired One , O Daughter of the Mountain (Parvati) .


The Compassionate Victor: Even in the midst of battle, the Goddess protects those who sincerely surrender. Her victory is not driven by hatred but by Dharma.
  • Enemy Wives Seeking Refuge The vulnerable parts of ourselves that seek healing after destructive patterns collapse.
  • Abhaya (Fearlessness) Inner security that arises when we surrender to truth rather than ego.
  • The Trident (Trishula) The power of wisdom that pierces ignorance across body, mind, and intellect.
  • Celestial Drums The inner joy and clarity that naturally arise when a major inner conflict is resolved.
  • The Core Lesson True strength is not merely defeating an enemy; it is remaining compassionate even after victory.

✍️ रक्ष रक्ष जगन्माते सर्वशक्ते जय दुर्गे।🙏

Many people believe strength means never showing compassion toward those who have wronged them. This verse presents a higher ideal. Durga defeats evil decisively, yet grants protection to those who sincerely surrender.

In our inner life, destructive habits, fears, and ego-driven desires often create suffering. When these tendencies weaken, the goal is not self-hatred or endless punishment. Instead, we acknowledge the lesson, learn from it, and move forward with wisdom.

The Goddess reminds us that true power includes both firmness and mercy. We can uphold boundaries without losing compassion.

********

Compassion is not weakness. It is the strength that remains after the battle has already been won.

Just as Durga's victory drums echo throughout the cosmos, inner peace naturally resonates through our lives when courage and compassion become one.
