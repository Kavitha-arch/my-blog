Mahishasura Mardhini

ॐ

Durga Contemplation


Maa Durga - The Feminine Energy which will protect us always

********* मन्त्रम् १***************

ॐ दुं दुर्गायै नमः

******** मन्त्रम् २****************

सर्वमंगल मांगल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थ साधिके।

शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते॥,

******** मन्त्रम् ३****************

या देवी सर्वभूतेषु शक्ति-रूपेण संस्थिता।

नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः॥

“मननात् त्रायते इति मन्त्रः”

A mantra is that which, through sustained contemplation and repetition (mananāt), protects (trāyatē) the seeker from suffering, bondage, and the cycle of birth and death (saṃsāra).

मन्त्रम् १. The Daily Protection Mantra

I bow to the Divine Mother Durga, who protects her devotees and destroys all difficulties.

मन्त्रम् २. The Universal Blessing Mantra

Salutations to the auspicious one, who bestows all blessings, fulfills every desire, gives refuge, and holds three eyes. Oh Narayani, I bow to you.

मन्त्रम् ३. The Power and Energy Mantra

To the Goddess who resides in all beings in the form of power and divine energy, I bow to her, I bow to her, I bow to her again and again.
