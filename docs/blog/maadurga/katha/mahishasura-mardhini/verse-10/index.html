Mahishasura Mardhini

ॐ

Verse 10: जय जय जप्य


10. Maa Durga - The Divine Cosmic Dance as Ardhanārīśvara.

जय जय जप्य जयेजयशब्द परस्तुति तत्परविश्वनुते

झणझणझिञ्झिमि झिङ्कृत नूपुरशिञ्जितमोहित भूतपते ।

नटित नटार्ध नटी नट नायक नाटितनाट्य सुगानरते

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १० ॥

10.1: Victory to You, victory to You, O Mother whose name is worthy of constant meditation, whose praises are sung with cries of 'Victory! Victory!', and who is adored and worshipped by the entire universe and who chant your supreme praise as hymns.

10.2: The enchanting sound of Your anklets, ringing with musical jingling tones, captivates even Lord Shiva, the Lord of the Ghosts and Goblins.

10.3: You delight in the divine dance, performing gracefully with the Lord whose half is the Divine Dancer (Shiva), rejoicing as the supreme dancer, the leader of all dancers, and the lover of sacred dance and melodious music.

10.4: Victory, victory to You, the Slayer of Mahishasura, the Beautiful One with lovely tresses, and the Daughter of the Mountain. May Your triumph be eternal.


The Fusion of Purusha and Prakriti
  • Purusha (Shiva) Pure, unmanifest consciousness. It is the silent witness.
  • Prakriti (Shivaa/Shakti) Primordial energy, nature, and matter. It is the driving force that gives form to consciousness.
  • The Significance The dance shows that consciousness (Shiva) is motionless without energy (Shakti), and energy is blind without direction from consciousness

✍️ रक्ष रक्ष जगन्माते सर्वशक्ते जय दुर्गे।🙏

The performance serves as a visual metaphor for the non-dual nature of ultimate reality (Advaita). While human perception breaks the world down into binaries—good and bad, male and female, light and dark—the Ardhanarishwara dance shows that these are merely two sides of the same single coin.
