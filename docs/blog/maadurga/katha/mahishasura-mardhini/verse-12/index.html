Verse 12: सहितमहाहव


12. Maa Durga - CO-EXISITNG OF COURAGE AND PEACE

सहितमहाहव मल्लमतल्लिक मल्लितरल्लक मल्लरते

विरचितवल्लिक पल्लिकमल्लिक झिल्लिकभिल्लिक वर्गवृते ।

शितकृतफुल्ल समुल्लसितारुण तल्लजपल्लव सल्ललिते

जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥ १२ ॥

12.1: O Divine Goddess engaged in a fierce cosmic battle, accompanied by a great war, who fights against the best of wrestlers and agile, fierce warriors, and who delights in subduing such mighty opponents.

12.2: You are beautifully adorned with creeping vines, dwelling near small forest hamlets, surrounded by wild jasmine flowers and the humming of crickets, and ever encircled by the tribal communities of forest dwellers.

12.3: Your divine form is charmingly graceful, radiant like highly polished and sharpened weapons, blooming beautifully like a fully blossomed flower, and splendidly shining with the reddish glow of the rising sun on excellent tender leaves.

12.4: Victory, victory to You, O Slayer of the demon Mahishasura, the beautiful-haired Daughter of the Mountain!


The Big Picture Integration.

When you put these sound cues together (Ranat → Lasat → Kanat), the stotram is purposefully leading your mind through a complete cycle:

  • Metallic Battlefield Clanging (Ranat)→ {Gentle Forest Swaying (Lasat)}→ Blinding Divine Radiance (Kanat)}
  • It reminds the chanter that the ultimate cosmic power encompasses the raw noise of life's battles, the absolute innocence of nature, and the unshakeable inner light of the Self all at once.
Spiritual Realization.
  • The Synthesis of Power and Purity
  • This illustrates that both courage and innocence can stay together. It is not necessary that when we engage in the battles of life, we forget to realize the beauty and innocence around us and within us. The forest dwellers and wild flowers symbolize the natural state of our true Self, which needs to invoke Durga at the time of a fight.

✍️ रक्ष रक्ष जगन्माते सर्वशक्ते जय दुर्गे।🙏

Protecting your spiritual home (the innocent forest within) is what gives you the pristine clarity and courage to successfully fight your physical battles outside.
